Questions, answered

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. 30 questions a month, flashcards and the basic planner, with no card and no trial countdown.

Can I change or cancel my plan later?

Any time, from Settings → Plan & billing. Cancelling keeps your access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Do you offer a student discount?

Verify a school or university email in Settings and 40% comes off any paid plan automatically.

Will this do my homework for me?

No — and that is on purpose. Study mode walks you through the method and asks you questions back, and full essay generation is blocked by default.

What happens to my data?

Your sessions stay yours. You can export everything or clear your history whenever you like from the privacy settings.

Is there a plan for schools?

Yes, classroom and department licences are available. Get in touch and we will put something together.
