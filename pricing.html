Yes. 30 questions a month and the basic planner, with no card and no trial countdown.

No — and that is on purpose. Study mode walks you through the method and asks you questions back, and full essay generation is blocked by default.

What happens to my data?

Your sessions stay yours. You can export everything or clear your history whenever you like from the privacy settings.
